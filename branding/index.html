Direction

No slow-motion background video.

Distinctive without becoming theatrical.

The visual system uses composition, typography, real photography, and restrained motion rather than a generic church template.

Warm and grounded

Real people, real places, understandable language, and practical hospitality.

Editorial and calm

Generous space, meaningful hierarchy, and confident but restrained use of color.

Explicitly Christian

Jesus and the Gospel remain clear without turning every paragraph into institutional promotion.

Color

A paper-like foundation with purposeful accents.

Neutrals do most of the work. Blue carries action and trust; sage, clay, gold, and cedar add specific warmth.

Elements

Simple actions and quiet surfaces.

The interface should support the invitation instead of competing with it.

Editorial card

Use cards only when grouping helps understanding. Avoid turning every section into a grid of interchangeable boxes.

Motion

Animated still images, not background video.

Motion belongs primarily in the homepage hero. The remainder of the page stays calm.

Behavior

  • Gentle movement and crossfades
  • Stable layout during transitions
  • Pause when the page is hidden
  • Useful without JavaScript
  • Static composition for reduced motion

Platform roles

One invitation, clear destinations.

The public website explains why a next step matters, then sends the person to the system responsible for that step.

Planning Center
